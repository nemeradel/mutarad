Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Mutarad Gaming ("we") runs the YouTube channel @mutarad and the Instagram/TikTok accounts @mutarad_gaming. "Mutarad Studio" is an internal tool used only by the channel owner to upload and manage the channel's own videos through the YouTube Data API.

What the tool accesses

The tool is authorised only by the channel owner. It uploads videos, sets thumbnails, playlists and descriptions, and reads the channel's own statistics and comments in order to publish content and reply to comments. It does not access any other user's account.

Data storage

Access tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own computer. We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties. We do not collect personal data from viewers beyond what YouTube shows publicly to channel owners (public comments and aggregate statistics).

YouTube API Services

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time from Google security settings.
